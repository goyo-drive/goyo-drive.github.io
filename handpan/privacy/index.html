Privacy Policy

Handpan Studio for iPhone and iPad | Effective date: October 25, 2026

This policy explains what information the Handpan Studio app ("the app") handles, why, who else receives it, and the choices you have. The app is provided by goyo drive (sole proprietor: Minhyeong Yu) ("we", "us").

Summary

  • No developer account or login. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your data stays on your device: recordings, sheets, custom scales, practice log, map pins and jam sessions.
  • Microphone audio for the tuner and for recording a real handpan is processed on your device. The app does not upload it for analysis.
  • Ads (free version only): Google AdMob shows ads and may collect device and usage data for advertising, subject to your consent choices and the iOS tracking setting.
  • AI composition (Pro, when the cloud service is available): your instrument name and note layout, the options you pick and your optional request text are sent, through our server, to Anthropic for processing. Our server uses your App Store transaction ID only to check your subscription and to limit request rates.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • The app is not directed to children under 13.

1. Information that stays on your device

The app stores local copies of the following on your device: scales you create, recordings, generated and edited sheets, exported PDFs and videos, your practice log and streaks, rhythm-game scores (also sent to Apple when you use Game Center), challenge stamps, map pins and jam sessions, and your settings. The developer does not receive these local files. It is removed when you delete the app, except for copies in device backups that you control through Apple.

When you share a PDF, video, image or file, the iOS share sheet sends it to the app or person you choose. We don't receive a copy.

Location. If you tap the location button on the map, iOS asks for permission and the app uses your current location only to center the map. The app does not store your location or send it to the developer's server. Apple Maps provides map tiles and place search under Apple's privacy policy.

Game Center (optional). If you sign in to Apple's Game Center and use rankings, your rhythm-game and challenge scores are sent to Game Center under your Game Center profile. Game Center is run by Apple and Apple's privacy policy applies; we don't receive your Game Center account details.

2. Microphone

The app asks for microphone access for the tuner, the instrument scan and recording a real handpan. The sound is analyzed in real time on your device. Recordings are saved on your device only when you choose to save them. The app does not upload microphone audio for analysis or AI composition. If you choose to share a saved recording, the iOS share sheet sends the file to the service you select. You can turn microphone access off at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone; the other features keep working.

3. Advertising with Google AdMob (free version)

If you don't have Handpan Pro, the app shows ads served by Google LLC through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Ads are not shown to Pro users.

What Google may collect through the app. According to Google's data disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK: your IP address (which may be used to estimate the general location of your device), device identifiers (the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking, otherwise identifiers limited to the app or developer), information about the ads you see and interact with, product interactions such as app launches and taps, crash logs and performance data such as launch time.

Why. To show ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and abuse, produce analytics and, only where you allow it, personalize ads.

Your choices.

  • Tracking (iOS): the App Tracking Transparency prompt lets you allow or deny tracking. You can change it later in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you deny it, you still see ads, but they are not personalized using your advertising identifier.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): before personalized ads are shown, the app displays Google's consent message (Google User Messaging Platform, IAB Transparency and Consent Framework). You can review or change your choices at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy choices.
  • US state privacy laws: where Google's message offers it, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising.
  • No ads: any Handpan Pro purchase removes ads.

Learn how Google uses this information at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and read Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. AI composition (Handpan Pro)

Cloud AI composition is optional and works only when the cloud service is available. If the app uses its offline exercise generator instead, the generated exercise stays on your device. When you request cloud AI composition:

  • What is sent: the name and note layout of the instrument you selected (note names and their positions), the options you chose (for example length, mood, difficulty, tempo and time signature) and the text you type in the optional request field. Please don't include personal information in that text.
  • How it travels: the app sends this over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our server. Our server forwards it to Anthropic, PBC, which runs the Claude AI model, and returns the generated piece to the app.
  • What is not sent: your name, contact details, location, device identifiers, recordings, audio or any other data stored in the app.
  • Subscription check: the app also sends the App Store transaction ID of your Pro purchase. Our server uses it only to confirm with Apple that your Pro access is active and to apply rate limits. It is not passed to Anthropic and is not used for advertising.
  • Retention on our server: we don't keep your request text or the generated piece after the reply is sent. To enforce rate limits we store purchase-verification records keyed by transaction identifiers for 15 minutes and daily usage records keyed by the original transaction identifier for up to 2 days. The server and Cloudflare process network metadata, including the IP address, to deliver requests and protect the service. Technical-log retention follows the Cloudflare service settings and policies; this app does not specify a separate retention period.
  • Anthropic: Anthropic processes the content as our service provider under its commercial terms. By default Anthropic does not use API inputs or outputs to train its models and deletes them within 30 days, unless longer retention is needed to enforce its usage policy or required by law. See https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy.
  • Your permission: the app asks for your permission before your first AI request. Everything else in the app works without AI composition.

5. Purchases

Handpan Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment; we don't receive your card details, name or contact information. Apple provides us with the transaction information needed to unlock your purchase (such as product and transaction identifiers) and aggregated sales reports. Apple's handling of your data is described at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

6. What we don't do

We don't create accounts, sell personal information, run our own analytics or tracking, or combine data from the app with data from other companies. If this changes, we will update this policy first.

7. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Personalized advertising: your consent.
  • Non-personalized advertising, fraud prevention and ad measurement: our legitimate interest in funding the free version, and your consent where the law requires it.
  • AI composition and the subscription check: performing the service you request (contract), with your permission before the first request.
  • Server security logs and rate limits: our legitimate interest in keeping the service available and preventing abuse.

8. International transfers

Google and Anthropic process data in the United States and other countries. When cloud AI composition is enabled, Cloudflare, Inc. hosts the proxy on its global network. Processing locations may vary by request. For details about each provider's processing and international-transfer safeguards, see the provider's privacy policy, including Cloudflare at https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent and to complain to a data protection authority. We do not collect your real-world identity in the app and cannot usually match a transaction identifier to your name. If you send us your transaction ID, we will delete the rate-limit records linked to it. For data collected by Google or Anthropic, you can also contact them directly.

10. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. In Korea, we don't collect personal information from children under 14 without consent from a legal guardian. If you believe a child has sent personal information through AI composition, contact us and we will delete what we hold.

11. Security

All network requests use HTTPS. We keep as little data as possible, and the keys for our AI provider are kept on our server, never in the app.

12. Changes

We will post any change on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.

13. Contact

Questions or requests: 12ymh12@gmail.com

핸드팬 스튜디오(Handpan Studio) iPhone과 iPad용 앱 | 시행일: 2026년 10월 25일

고요드라이브(대표 유민형, 이하 "개발자")는 핸드팬 스튜디오 앱(이하 "앱")을 이용하는 분의 개인정보를 "개인정보 보호법" 등 관련 법령에 따라 처리하며, 다음과 같이 처리방침을 공개합니다.

요약

  • 개발자 계정에 가입할 필요가 없습니다. 이름, 이메일, 전화번호를 묻지 않습니다.
  • 녹음, 악보, 커스텀 스케일, 연습 기록, 지도 핀, 잼 세션은 기기에만 저장됩니다.
  • 마이크 소리(튜너, 실제 핸드팬 녹음)는 기기 안에서 처리되며 분석을 위해 서버에 업로드하지 않습니다.
  • 광고(무료 버전): Google AdMob이 광고를 표시하며, 동의 선택과 iOS 추적 설정에 따라 광고용 기기, 이용 정보를 수집할 수 있습니다.
  • AI 작곡(Pro, 클라우드 서비스 제공 시): 악기의 이름과 음 배열, 선택한 옵션, 직접 입력한 요청 문구(선택)가 개발자 서버를 거쳐 Anthropic에 전달됩니다. 서버는 App Store 거래 ID를 구독 확인과 요청 횟수 제한에만 사용합니다.
  • 결제는 Apple이 처리하며, 개발자는 결제 정보를 받지 않습니다.
  • 이 앱은 만 13세 미만 아동을 대상으로 하지 않습니다.

  • 광고 표시와 측정, 같은 광고의 반복 제한, 부정 클릭 방지, 통계, (동의한 경우) 맞춤형 광고
