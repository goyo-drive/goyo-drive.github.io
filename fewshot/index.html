Fewshot

Sort a few. AI sorts the rest.

Fewshot organizes your iPhone photo library into the classes you choose. Swipe through a few photos and tap a class. The AI on your iPhone learns from them and sorts the rest. Your photos never leave your device.

App Store Help & Support Privacy Policy

How it works

  • Swipe through your photos and tap a class: Food, Pets, Travel, Documents, or one you create.
  • Once each class has a few samples, the on-device AI (Google's EmbeddingGemma 2) sorts the rest of your library.
  • Swipe through the AI's picks to confirm or fix them. Every review makes it more accurate.
  • Pick a class in the Camera tab and shoot. New photos are saved already sorted.

몇 장만 분류하면 나머지는 AI가

Fewshot은 내가 정한 클래스대로 iPhone 사진 보관함을 정리해요. 사진 몇 장을 넘기며 클래스를 탭하면, iPhone 안의 AI가 그 사진들로 배워서 나머지를 분류해요. 사진은 기기 밖으로 나가지 않아요.
